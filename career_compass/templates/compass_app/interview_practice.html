Interview Practice Tips

Do's
  • Practice consistently, not just the day before
  • Understand concepts rather than memorizing answers
  • Time yourself to improve speed and efficiency
  • Review explanations for questions you get wrong
  • Focus on your weak areas but don't neglect strengths
Don'ts
  • Don't cram without understanding
  • Don't just focus on questions, understand the underlying concepts
  • Don't get discouraged by difficult questions
  • Don't ignore your progress metrics
  • Don't practice without simulating real interview conditions
